# Vale Household Rules

These rules and all referenced household records belong to the fictional prototype household.

## 1. Household decision authority

- Morgan Vale (`MEM-001`) is the household's authorized adult for family-rule decisions and persistent household updates.
- This authorization is part of the household's stored configuration. It does not change Streaming Guard's immutable advisory-only system boundary.

## 2. Monthly budget

- The household streaming budget cap is **$75 USD per month before any applicable tax**.
- A budget change must be stated explicitly by the authorized adult and scoped as temporary or permanent.

## 3. Content ratings

- Casey Vale (`MEM-004`, age 9) may be included only for television titles rated TV-G or TV-PG and movies rated G or PG.
- Riley Vale (`MEM-003`, age 15) may be included for television titles through TV-14 and movies through PG-13.
- TV-MA and R-rated titles must not be used to justify a subscription for a child when the title is on that child's watchlist or the child is an intended viewer.
- Do not apply a child's rating limit to a title solely because the child belongs to the household.
- If a title is associated with a child but the intended child viewers are unclear, request clarification before recommending an action based on the title.
- Only the authorized adult may approve a rating exception.
- Every exception applies only to one named title and the specifically named child viewer or viewers.
- A rating exception never permanently changes a child's rating limit and does not apply to another title, sequel, remake, season, service, genre, or future recommendation.

## 4. Household preferences

- The household currently weights high-priority titles more heavily than medium- or low-priority titles.
- When household preferences conflict, the authorized adult makes the final family decision.

## 5. Family-rule changes

- These are the Vale household's current baseline rules and are loaded into persistent household memory.
- The authorized adult may explicitly change the budget, priorities, acceptable waiting periods, advertising preferences, resolution preferences, or other household-specific rules.
- Child rating limits remain the default child-safety boundary. The authorized adult may approve only title-specific exceptions for named child viewers.
- A change must be stored with its scope (`one_time` or `permanent`), source, and timestamp.
- A child-rating exception must always use `one_time` scope.
- Silence, disagreement, a viewing request, or agreement with a recommendation does not change a family rule.
- A family-rule change cannot override Streaming Guard's immutable system boundaries, including its inability to perform external account actions or retain sensitive credentials.
- The authorized adult may add household-specific escalation conditions or later change or remove conditions the household added.
- Household escalation conditions are additive only. They cannot remove, disable, narrow, weaken, or override any escalation required by Streaming Guard's immutable system policy.

## 6. Additional household escalation conditions

- None currently recorded.
- This section stores only escalation conditions added by the household. The immutable system escalation policy is not stored here and cannot be edited by the household.
